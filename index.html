The trading routine

Four steps. Every trade.

01 / 04
01

Plan before action

Map important levels, define your scenarios and know what would invalidate the idea — before you click anything.

02

Wait for your setup

Let price come to your plan. Choosing not to trade is also a valid, disciplined decision.

03

Manage your risk

Size positions sensibly, set your stop-loss and protect your capital first. Survival makes consistency possible.

04

Review every trade

Record what happened, learn without emotion and refine the process for tomorrow’s session.

Then repeat.
That’s the whole secret.

    Core principles

    What we train for.

    Four principles sit under every lesson and every routine at Smart Trade.

    i.

    Market awareness

    Build an understanding of price movement and changing market conditions before forming an opinion.

    ii.

    Consistency

    Create repeatable habits and follow a considered trading routine — session after session.

    iii.

    Discipline

    Practice patience, preparation and emotional control in every decision you make.

    iv.

    Risk awareness

    Make risk part of the process, not an afterthought. Protect capital first; opportunity comes again.
